Care with Kantay

Easy care for your new plant 🌱

☀️

Light

Keep in indirect sunlight. In peak winters, direct sunlight gives the best results.

💧

Watering

Spray water generously the first time, then spray again only when you feel the soil has dried out.

🪴

Pour on the soil

Make sure to pour water on the soil, not on the plant.

🍃

Best spot

A well ventilated place with moderate sunlight shows the best growth.

🌱

Roots

It'll grow roots soon Insha'Allah. Until then, if it gets displaced, just put it back on the soil mix. No need to sow it.

🌧️

Rain

Put it outside for some time in the rain. It loves it :)
